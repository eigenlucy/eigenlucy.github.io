The Trans Continental Exodus

November 10, 2024 • Lucy M.

2024   ·   blog  

It’s been a wild month and a half. I left my job at Kyocera and started doing contract hardware design full time, our house got destroyed, I got married, I moved to San Francisco, all within about a month and a half. It’s been quite the emotional roller coaster, and I suspect my nerves look like fried wires, but it all worked out and it was all for the best!

In addition to the PCB design and full stack hardware development contracting, I have started a fellowship at atopile.io — expect to see lots of fun open source projects involving that, which you can follow on my GitHub @eigenlucy
